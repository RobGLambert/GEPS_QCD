How to read this

QCD = (Q3 − Q1) / (Q3 + Q1) of the 12 h accumulation across the GEPS members. Values near 0 mean the ensemble is tightly clustered (high agreement); values near 1 mean a wide spread relative to the amounts (low confidence). Areas with no forecast accumulation are left blank.
